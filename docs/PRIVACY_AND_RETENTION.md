# Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 Compliance & Data Governance

**Statutory Framework:** Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) & Applicable Indian Electoral Laws  
**Notice Identifier:** ElectionDesk DPDP Privacy Specification v2026.1  
**Appointed Officer:** Data Protection Officer (`dpo@electiondesk.gov`)

---

## 1. Compliance Disclaimer & Purpose Limitation

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Legal Notice:** This software is designed as an administrative and technological framework for managing electoral roll data in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Unless formally commissioned and authorized by the Election Commission of India (ECI) or a designated State Election Commission, this deployment must not be represented as an official Commission portal.

### Purpose Limitation
Personal data collected in ElectionDesk is processed solely for the legitimate use of:
1. Verifying voter registration eligibility and polling booth allocation.
2. Generating authorized electoral rolls and polling station rosters.
3. Conducting data quality reviews to eliminate duplicate registrations.

**Prohibited Processing:**
- Under no circumstances may voter records be processed to infer political opinions, profile voter preferences, target citizens for political persuasion, or expose secret ballot selections.
- Enrichment with scraped social media or third-party marketing datasets is strictly prohibited.

## 2. Minimum Necessary Data Inventory

| Field Category | Specific Fields Collected | Lawful Basis | Justification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Official Identity** | Full Name, Relative Name, Relation Type | Statutory Mandate | Required on official electoral rolls for citizen identity verification at polling stations. |
| **Electoral Identifier** | Official EPIC Number | Statutory Mandate | Unique primary key across state rolls to prevent duplicate voting. |
| **Demographics** | Gender, Age, Date of Birth | Statutory Mandate | Verifying voting eligibility (minimum 18 years of age) and gender quotas. |
| **Residential Address** | Door No., Street, Locality, City, PIN Code | Statutory Mandate | Establishing residence within the assembly constituency and determining polling booth assignment. |
| **Polling Association**| Booth Number, Station Name | Statutory Mandate | Mapping citizen to authorized physical polling station on polling day. |
| **Citizen Portrait** | Biometric Photograph (Optional) | Explicit Consent / Statutory | Visual photo-roll identity verification at polling station. |

## 3. Data Retention & Secure Erasure Schedule

1. **Active Electoral Records:**
   - Retained for the statutory duration of the elected body's term (typically 5 years / 1,825 days) or until officially updated during an annual summary revision.
2. **Archived Records (Soft-Deleted):**
   - Retained for a maximum of **90 days** in an isolated soft-delete state to permit administrative review of contested deletions.
   - Automatically purged permanently upon execution of the `/governance/purge` workflow.
3. **Audit Log Trail:**
   - Retained in tamper-evident hash-chained format for **7 years** for statutory compliance and electoral petition dispute resolution.
4. **Exported CSV Downloads:**
   - Temporary export files expire automatically within **2 hours** on the server.

## 4. Synthetic Data Enforcement in Development & Testing

- **Strict Prohibition:** Real citizen voter records must **NEVER** be imported into local development, staging, or testing environments.
- **Synthetic Test Generation:** All local development, unit tests, automated integration suites, and UI demonstrations must utilize synthetic records generated by `backend/tests/helpers/testFixtures.js`.
